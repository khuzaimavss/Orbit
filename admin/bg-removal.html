Admin / Background Removal

Clean up a product photo

Upload a raw photo and this tool strips a plain background out to white by flood-filling from the edges — good enough for quick, evenly-lit product shots. It's a lightweight color-based approach, not a trained segmentation model: busy or shadowed backgrounds may need a higher threshold, and true studio-grade cutouts are better served by a dedicated API (e.g. remove.bg or Photoroom) wired up on a backend.
